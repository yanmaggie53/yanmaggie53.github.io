2025 MIT IMES Data visualization

ZzAlign Patient Dashboards

Interactive data dashboards supporting research on the ZzAlign mouthguard, a device for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). The dashboards bring multimodal sleep, device, and survey data into a single, centralized interface so researchers can explore and analyze it efficiently.
