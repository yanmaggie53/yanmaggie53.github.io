About

I work in human–computer and human–AI interaction, with a focus on trust in health and decision-making.

I’m a designer and researcher studying Media Arts & Sciences (computer science + art and design) at Wellesley College, and doing research at the MIT Media Lab. I’m interested in AI trust, co-creation, and usability in health and decision-making contexts.
